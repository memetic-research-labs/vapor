## Rules for Forks and Derivatives

If you fork, modify, or redistribute the Vapor source code:

- You **must remove** the Vapor logo and app icon from your distribution
- You **must replace** them with your own original branding
- You **must not** use the name "Vapor" in your project name, domain, app name, or any branding
- You **must not** use any branding that could be confused with the official Vapor project

## Permitted Use

The following uses are permitted without prior written consent:

- **Referential use** — Describing your project as "compatible with Vapor", "built for Vapor", or "based on Vapor source code" (provided it does not imply endorsement)
- **Documentation and education** — Using the Vapor name or screenshots in blog posts, tutorials, talks, or academic papers for the purpose of describing or reviewing the project
- **Upstream contributions** — Using the Vapor brand within contributions submitted to the official repository

## Prohibited Use

The following are prohibited without explicit written permission from Memetic Research Labs LLC:

- Using the Vapor name, logo, or app icon in your own product or service branding
- Distributing a modified version of Vapor under the Vapor name or with Vapor branding
- Using Vapor branding on merchandise, websites, or marketing materials
- Implying endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with Memetic Research Labs LLC
- Using Vapor trademarks in domain names or social media handles
